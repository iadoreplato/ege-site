# variant-1-t1: Learning something new: six opinions
Задание 1, юнит 1, модуль variant

## Текст записи
**Announcer:** Speaker A

**Speaker A:** When I bought a guitar, I thought I'd practise for hours every weekend. That lasted two weeks. So I tried ten minutes every day, even when my fingers hurt. A year later I can play twelve songs, though I still make mistakes every evening. My brother has one long session on Sundays and nothing else, and he's still on the same three chords.

**Announcer:** Speaker B

**Speaker B:** I'd never have kept going with Spanish at home with a video app, because my motivation lasted two weeks each time. Then I joined an evening class with eight strangers. I was embarrassed by my accent at first, but theirs were worse, and we laughed. When I felt like skipping a lesson, I remembered that the group would ask where I was. Last week I ordered a whole dinner in Spanish.

**Announcer:** Speaker C

**Speaker C:** My first programme crashed eleven times in a row, and I nearly gave up. My teacher said every mistake was telling me what I'd misunderstood, so I wrote them all in a notebook and read it every day for a month. It turned out to be the most useful book I own. Now, when something goes wrong, I'm almost pleased.

**Announcer:** Speaker D

**Speaker D:** My daughters laughed when I said I wanted to learn to swim at fifty-two. For years I was too embarrassed to mention it, because beginners are supposed to be children. Then I found an instructor with pupils from six to seventy-eight. I'm slow, and I always will be, but last summer I swam across a lake.

**Announcer:** Speaker E

**Speaker E:** Photography began as my hobby on holiday. Friends started asking me to take pictures at their weddings, and a local magazine found my website. Two years later I left my office job. People ask whether I learnt from video lessons. A few, but mostly I took one picture every day.

**Announcer:** Speaker F

**Speaker F:** I watched hundreds of cooking video lessons before I made bread. In every one it looked easy. My first loaf was like a brick, but everybody makes mistakes. Then my neighbour, who bakes professionally, stood next to me for one afternoon and showed me how the dough should feel. I learnt more in three hours than in all my evenings online.

## Задание
- 1. Beginners should not be embarrassed about their level.
- 2. Learning in a group keeps you motivated.
- 3. A hobby can turn into a job.
- 4. Video lessons are not enough to learn a practical skill.
- 5. Mistakes are a useful part of learning.
- 6. A little practice every day works better than long sessions once a week.
- 7. You are never too old to learn something new.

**Ключ:** A–6, B–2, C–5, D–7, E–3, F–4
