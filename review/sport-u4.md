# sport-u4: Traditional sports: six opinions
Задание 1, юнит 4, модуль sport

## Текст записи
**Announcer:** Speaker A

**Speaker A:** My grandad says lapta is an old man's game, and he's the only one in our family who ever mentions it. I've never seen it on television, not once, and I do check. Sports channels show the same three sports on repeat. My friends would rather watch football on their phones, and I can't blame them.

**Announcer:** Speaker B

**Speaker B:** My grandfather played gorodki on this very field, and so did his father, but that isn't why I play. When I throw the bat, I'm doing what people in this country have done for hundreds of years, and no other country has this game. Museums look after old things, but a traditional game stays alive only while somebody plays it.

**Announcer:** Speaker C

**Speaker C:** I've taught PE for eleven years, and most of my pupils have never held a bat. Some parents show their children traditional games like lapta at weekends, but many work too much. So I've put lapta and gorodki into the timetable, one lesson a month. If schools don't teach them, I don't think anybody will.

**Announcer:** Speaker D

**Speaker D:** Our Saturday group in the park has players from nine to seventy-six. People think traditional games are for retired gentlemen, and half of our regulars are indeed retired. But last week a nine-year-old and a seventy-six-year-old played in the same team, and it was the nine-year-old who threw the winning bat.

**Announcer:** Speaker E

**Speaker E:** When I started gorodki, I couldn't stand losing. I'd throw, miss and shout. My coach, a quiet man in his sixties, kept saying 'Be patient, breathe, look at the target.' It took months. Last week I managed to control my temper through a very long exam, and my mother asked what had happened to me.

**Announcer:** Speaker F

**Speaker F:** Nobody at school ever taught me gorodki; we only had football and volleyball. My grandfather put a bat in my hand in his garden one August, then my father joined in, then my uncle. I learnt the game from my relatives, and I've never read the rules. I just watched them.

## Задание
- 1. Traditional games are mainly for the older generation.
- 2. Traditional games are part of what makes a country different.
- 3. Traditional games bring different age groups together.
- 4. Traditional games teach children patience and self-control.
- 5. Schools should teach traditional games.
- 6. Children usually learn traditional games from relatives.
- 7. Television shows traditional sports very rarely.

**Ключ:** A–7, B–2, C–5, D–3, E–4, F–6
