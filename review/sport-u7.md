# sport-u7: How young people spend their free time: six opinions
Задание 1, юнит 7, модуль sport

## Текст записи
**Announcer:** Speaker A

**Speaker A:** I'd wanted to learn the guitar for years, but I never lasted more than twenty minutes with a video tutorial on my laptop. Then my cousin and two classmates started a band. We're terrible, and we meet on Thursdays whether we're ready or not. Last month I finally played a whole song from beginning to end, and the three of them cheered as if it were a concert.

**Announcer:** Speaker B

**Speaker B:** Last Saturday I watched short videos for four hours, and I remember nothing. While it was happening it felt like rest. Afterwards I had a headache and the feeling that I'd lost an evening. When I spend the same four hours baking bread or cycling to the lake, I come home tired, but I feel better for days.

**Announcer:** Speaker C

**Speaker C:** On Sundays I lie on the sofa and look at the ceiling. No phone, no plans. For a long time I felt I ought to be learning Spanish or something, because everybody around me is always busy. Now I protect that afternoon, and on Mondays I'm a different person. It's the only real rest I get.

**Announcer:** Speaker D

**Speaker D:** Fixing bikes began as a way to avoid my maths revision. Spare parts cost a lot of money, but there's a scrapyard near us where you can pick up almost anything for pocket money. Last summer a shop owner saw me mending my neighbour's wheel and offered me a Saturday job. Now I'm applying to study mechanical engineering.

**Announcer:** Speaker E

**Speaker E:** At school I get average marks and sit at the back. On Wednesday evenings at the art studio there are twelve of us, and we learn from one another. They call me 'the one who paints the weird skies'. It's silly, but at school nobody has ever called me anything, and at the studio I'm somebody.

**Announcer:** Speaker F

**Speaker F:** After football, homework and a Spanish class, it's nine o'clock and my brain simply switches off. At weekends I mostly sleep. My grandparents talk about long afternoons doing whatever they liked, which sounds like science fiction to me. I've got a list of hobbies I'd love to try, from pottery to photography, but classes cost money too, and there's no free time in my week.

## Задание
- 1. Watching videos for hours leaves you feeling worse.
- 2. A hobby can turn into a career.
- 3. Doing nothing is a good way to rest.
- 4. Hobbies cost too much money for many families.
- 5. It is more fun to learn something new with other people.
- 6. Teenagers have no free time because they are too busy.
- 7. A hobby can give you a place where you are somebody special.

**Ключ:** A–5, B–1, C–3, D–2, E–7, F–6
