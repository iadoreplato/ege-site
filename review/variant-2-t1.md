# variant-2-t1: Lessons from travelling: six opinions
Задание 1, юнит 2, модуль variant

## Текст записи
**Announcer:** Speaker A

**Speaker A:** Before Portugal I spent a month learning a few words from an app, and I felt silly saying them aloud on the first morning. The baker laughed, then gave me a free pastry. My pronunciation was terrible. But after 'good morning' and 'thank you' in their language, people started talking to me, in the market, in taxis, even in somebody's kitchen.

**Announcer:** Speaker B

**Speaker B:** The first time I went abroad, I packed three pairs of boots and a hairdryer. The airline lost my suitcase for four days, and I managed with the small bag I'd kept with me. The trip also cost much less than planned, because I had nothing to put souvenirs in. Since then I take one small bag. I walk past baggage reclaim and I'm in the city before the others.

**Announcer:** Speaker C

**Speaker C:** Our train broke down in the middle of nowhere, on the way to a famous castle. The driver told us about a village two kilometres away, so we walked there, not very happily. It was market day: music, grandmothers selling honey, a man showing us his old bicycles. We changed all our plans and never saw the castle. I didn't take a single photo, and I still remember that afternoon.

**Announcer:** Speaker D

**Speaker D:** Guidebooks tell you where to eat, but not what local people are worried about or what makes them laugh. In Georgia I stayed with a family. I knew five words of the language, so we mostly used English and our hands. Their daughter was my age, and at breakfast she explained the school system and the jokes everybody tells. I did the museums too, and I remember them less.

**Announcer:** Speaker E

**Speaker E:** A lot of my friends think a good trip means a nice hotel and a rented car. I went around Scotland with a tent, a bicycle and about two hundred pounds. I washed in rivers and cooked on a tiny stove. In the rain it was hard, and I carried everything on my back, but I met more interesting people on campsites than in any resort. I still tell those stories every week.

**Announcer:** Speaker F

**Speaker F:** Six of us went to Italy last year. By the third day we were arguing about everything: Francesca wanted museums, Tom wanted the beach, and I wanted lunch. Our plan fell apart on the second morning. In the end we took turns choosing the day, and I liked the museums more than I'd expected.

## Задание
- 1. Travelling with only a small bag makes a trip easier.
- 2. Meeting local people shows you what a place is really like.
- 3. A change of plan can become the best part of a trip.
- 4. In a group you must sometimes give way to others.
- 5. A cheap trip can be as good as an expensive one.
- 6. It pays to know some basic phrases in the local language.
- 7. Photos can never replace real memories.
