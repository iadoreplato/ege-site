# variant-2-t2: A surprise party: Maya and Ben make plans
Задание 2, юнит 2, модуль variant

## Текст записи
**Maya:** Ben, thanks for coming. I wanted to talk about Lucy's party before the others arrive.

**Ben:** Sure. Her birthday is on the twenty-first, right? I thought we'd just go out for pizza.

**Maya:** That's what I'd planned originally, but then her sister told me Lucy is expecting exactly that, so there wouldn't be any surprise.

**Ben:** Fair point. What's the new idea?

**Maya:** Her aunt has a garden house just outside town. It's empty in October, and she says we can use it for free, as long as we clean it afterwards.

**Ben:** Free is good. Is it big enough?

**Maya:** For about twenty people. I've counted: there'll be fifteen if everybody from her class comes, plus a few from the swimming club.

**Ben:** What about getting there? It's not on a bus route, is it?

**Maya:** There's a bus until six, but after that, nothing. My dad has offered to drive some of us in his van, and I'm sure other parents will help.

**Ben:** Brilliant. And food?

**Maya:** That's your department, I thought. You're the one who made that enormous chocolate cake for the school fair.

**Ben:** Ha! It collapsed halfway through the fair, you know. But I'll try again with a different recipe. Should I do a cake with candles, or is that too childish?

**Maya:** She'll love it. Her favourite is lemon, by the way, not chocolate, so keep that in mind.

**Ben:** Lemon. Noted. What about music? I could bring my speakers.

**Maya:** Tom has promised to put together a playlist, but the speakers would be useful, because the ones in the house are ancient.

**Ben:** No problem. How are we going to get Lucy there without her suspecting?

**Maya:** Her sister will tell her that the family are going for a walk in the countryside. When they arrive at the garden house, all of us will be hiding inside.

**Ben:** And if she guesses?

**Maya:** She won't. She guessed last time because somebody posted a photo online, so I've asked everybody not to put anything on social media until the party is over.

**Ben:** Sensible. Are we buying a present together?

**Maya:** I'd like us to give one big present rather than twenty small ones. I'm thinking of a good camera, since she's always wanted to take up photography. We'd need about eight pounds each.

**Ben:** That's fine. I'll collect the money from the boys.

**Maya:** Thank you. I'll send everyone the details this evening.

## Задание
- A. Maya had first intended to celebrate Lucy's birthday with a pizza meal.  → **True**
- B. Lucy's aunt expects to be paid for the use of her garden house.  → **False**
- C. Every guest can reach the garden house by bus.  → **False**
- D. Ben has previously baked a cake for a school event.  → **True**
- E. Lucy's family are planning a separate dinner on the day of her birthday.  → **Not stated**
- F. The guests are going to give Lucy several small gifts.  → **False**
- G. Tom will play the music at the party himself.  → **Not stated**
