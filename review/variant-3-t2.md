# variant-3-t2: Weekend plans: Chloe and Sam prepare a camping trip
Задание 2, юнит 3, модуль variant

## Текст записи
**Chloe:** Sam, have you looked at the weather forecast for next weekend?

**Sam:** I have, and it's not great. Rain on Saturday, and then it clears up on Sunday afternoon.

**Chloe:** Hmm. Should we postpone the camping trip?

**Sam:** I'd rather not. The booking at the campsite cost a fortune, and they don't give refunds. Besides, Max and Olga have already taken the days off.

**Chloe:** Fair enough. In that case we need a proper tent. Does yours still leak?

**Sam:** Not any more. My uncle repaired it last summer, and I tested it in the garden during that storm in August. Not a drop came through.

**Chloe:** Good. What about sleeping bags? Mine is a summer one, so I'll freeze.

**Sam:** Mine is the same. But my cousin has two spare winter ones. I'm going to pick them up on Thursday.

**Chloe:** Lovely. And food? Last time we lived on sandwiches, and I never want to eat another one.

**Sam:** Agreed. I thought we could take a small gas stove and cook pasta. Max is bringing sausages for the first evening.

**Chloe:** Max? He can't even boil an egg.

**Sam:** He says he's been practising, and he's bought a special pan. We'll see.

**Chloe:** What about the route? The campsite is by the lake, isn't it? Is it far from the car park?

**Sam:** About three kilometres, but it's an easy path through the forest. The guidebook says it takes under an hour with backpacks.

**Chloe:** Good. I've also been reading about the wildlife. Apparently there are wild boar in that forest.

**Sam:** Yes, but they're shy and usually keep away from people. The warden's advice is to keep all food in a closed container and not to leave rubbish around.

**Chloe:** Sensible. Oh, and I'll bring my camera. There's supposed to be a view from the hill behind the campsite that's incredible at sunrise.

**Sam:** Then we'll have to get up early. I'm not good at that.

**Chloe:** I'll wake you. I've already set the alarm on my phone for half past five.

**Sam:** Half past five! You're mad. Fine, but I'm not making breakfast before eight.

**Chloe:** Deal. Right, I'll make a checklist and send it to everyone tonight.

**Sam:** Great. And don't forget the first-aid kit this time.

## Задание
- A. Rain is forecast for the whole weekend.  → **False**
- B. The campsite will not give money back if the group cancels.  → **True**
- C. Sam's tent no longer lets water in.  → **True**
- D. Chloe already has a sleeping bag suitable for cold nights.  → **False**
- E. Max has taken a cooking course.  → **Not stated**
- F. The campsite has hot showers.  → **Not stated**
- G. Chloe intends to take pictures of the landscape at first light.  → **True**
